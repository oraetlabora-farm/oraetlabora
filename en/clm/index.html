About the farm ES·EN

A network of family farms

Part of the Catholic Land Movement

OraEtLabora.farm is part of the Catholic Land Movement, an international network of Catholic families who want to return to the land and live their faith in the countryside. We do not walk alone.

The movement

Back to the land, together

The Catholic Land Movement helps Catholic families take the step towards a life in the countryside, on productive land of their own, and not to take it alone.

Its roots lie in England in the 1930s, when G. K. Chesterton, Hilaire Belloc and the Dominican Vincent McNabb encouraged families to go back to the land. Today the movement has been renewed and is organised in local chapters in the United States, Europe, Latin America, Australia and Africa, where families help one another, learn together and share their faith.

This is not just about moving to the countryside. It is about recovering what has been lost: real contact with the land, with one another and with God.

Catholic Land Movement

Its four pillars

The same ones we live here

When we came across the movement, we recognised ourselves in it. Its four pillars match what we were already seeking on the farm.

Resettlement

Back to the land

The movement supports families who want to live in the countryside, on land of their own. We have already taken that step: the farm was born from the search for a life closer to reality, set by the sun, the seasons and what the land asks of us.

On the farm · Terra and Creatio: we farm organically and care for our animals, because the land is God’s work and we are only its stewards.

Education

Learning the crafts

The movement trains people in the skills of the farm and the home: the garden, animals, carpentry, preserving food and traditional crafts.

On the farm · Studium and the workshop: we study the natural and agrarian arts, history, the Word of God and technology, and in the workshop we work wood and iron.

Fellowship

Not doing it alone

No farm stands on its own. The movement brings families and farms together to support one another in practical, spiritual and intellectual matters.

On the farm · Communio and Harmonia: we gather families and friends around the table and for celebrations, and we look after the harmony in which everyone has their place.

Glorifying God

Christ at the centre

The movement seeks to place Christ at the centre of rural life, through prayer, the liturgy and the sacraments, and to offer each day’s work to God.

On the farm · Oratio: it is the heart of our name, pray and work: prayer gives meaning to work, and work makes prayer more real.

With the network

Small things, done with great love

The movement invites people to start where they are, with what they have: a tree planted with a neighbour, a shared meal, a child learning where food comes from.

That is how we want to live our membership: welcoming families from the movement who would like to see life on the land, sharing what we learn in the garden, with the animals and in the workshop, and praying and working together on days of shared prayer and work.
